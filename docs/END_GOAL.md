# sv3.network

## 🌐 Vision

sv3.network is the **Web3-native evolution of SignVault.io** — a decentralized document, signature, and compliance platform that turns documents into **on-chain Real-World Assets (RWAs)**. This is a Web3 Feature track for **[SignVault.io](https://www.signvault.io)**. Our mission is to redefine how enterprises and teams handle trust, compliance, and collaboration in the digital age.

---

## 🚀 End Goal

To build a **fully decentralized, blockchain-native compliance and document management network**, where:

1. **User Identity & Access Control**

   * Every user is represented on-chain with wallet-based identity (no email/password).
   * Supports integration with decentralized identity standards (ENS, Lens, DID).
   * Fine-grained role-based and attribute-based access control recorded on-chain.

2. **Documents as On-Chain RWAs**

   * Every document is minted as an NFT (ERC-721 for unique docs, ERC-1155 for multi-instance docs).
   * Document metadata includes: file hash, IPFS/Arweave storage pointer, timestamp, owner, and organization ID.
   * Real-world documents like contracts, agreements, certifications become blockchain-verified RWAs.

3. **E-Signatures on Chain**

   * Signatures are recorded as on-chain events, tied directly to signer wallet addresses.
   * Timestamped, immutable, and verifiable by third parties.
   * Multi-signature workflows supported (e.g., all board members sign before document is valid).

4. **Organizations & Teams On-Chain**

   * Organizations and teams represented as smart contract objects.
   * Memberships tied to wallet addresses, roles, and access rights.
   * Transparent governance and accountability with auditable records.

5. **Audit & Compliance Layer**

   * Immutable event log for every document action (creation, modification, signature, revocation).
   * APIs for regulators, auditors, and compliance officers to query proof of compliance.
   * Drastically reduces audit costs and manual verification processes.

6. **Interoperability**

   * Works across any EVM-compatible chain (ETH, Base, AVAX, Monad, Polygon, etc.).
   * Multi-chain deployment with bridges for document ownership and verification portability.
   * Ensures enterprises can adopt the chain that fits their cost and performance requirements.

7. **Privacy & Zero-Knowledge Proofs**

   * Sensitive document data can be verified using zk-proofs without exposing content.
   * Enables compliance checks (e.g., verifying a license exists) without revealing full documents.

8. **Enterprise Integrations**

   * SDKs and APIs for enterprises to integrate sv3.network into their workflows.
   * Connectors for ERP, CRM, HR, and legacy compliance systems.
   * Hybrid deployment models (on-chain + private IPFS clusters for enterprises).
